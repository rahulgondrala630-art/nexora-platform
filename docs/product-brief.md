# Nexora Product Brief

## Problem
Teams lose momentum when decisions, ownership, and delivery signals are scattered across chats, documents, and task lists. Existing dashboards often report activity without helping a person decide what to do next.

## Users
- Product and engineering leads who need a calm view of delivery health.
- Individual contributors who need context without a meeting.
- Small teams that want visibility before adopting heavyweight planning software.

## Product promise
Nexora makes the next useful action obvious without hiding the context behind it.

## Success measures
- A first-time user can identify the two workstreams needing attention in under 60 seconds.
- Every workstream has a visible owner, status, priority, and progress signal.
- A decision can be traced from activity context to the affected workstream.
- The interface remains usable at mobile widths and with keyboard navigation.

## Non-goals for the first release
Real-time collaboration, billing, external integrations, and multi-tenant authorization are intentionally staged after the core workflow is proven.
